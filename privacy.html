3. Information we collect

3.1 Information you provide

  • Account information: email address, display name, profile photo or avatar (if you add one), authentication identifiers from sign-in providers (e.g. Apple, email/password via Amazon Cognito).
  • Poker hand data: game format, blinds, positions, stacks, hole cards, board cards, action sequences, outcomes, tags, tournament field info, and optional notes you enter.
  • Session metadata: region or location labels you select, session names, dates and times of hands.
  • Community activity: hands you choose to share, likes or comments (if enabled), support messages you send us.
  • Purchases: subscription or in-app purchase status (we receive confirmation from Apple; we do not receive your full payment card number).

3.2 Information collected automatically

  • Device and app data: device type, operating system version, app version, language, crash logs, and diagnostic data.
  • Usage data: feature usage (e.g. filters used, sync events).
  • Network and security: IP address, approximate region derived from IP, timestamps, and security logs for fraud prevention and API rate limiting.

Hands may include player names, nicknames, or notes about other people at the table. You are responsible for having a lawful basis to record that information. We process it only to provide the Service to you and, where applicable, as part of aggregated statistics or shared content you publish.

5. Community statistics vs. sharing (important distinction)

5.1 Aggregated community statistics

When “Contribute to community statistics” is enabled in Settings, synced hands may be used to update anonymous aggregate counters (for example: sample size, average net big blinds, preflop raise frequency, continuation-bet frequency for a spot such as “button open vs big blind”).

For this purpose we aim to use a scrubbed analytics envelope, including for example:

  • Game format and type
  • Hero and villain positions (not real names)
  • Preflop pot type, action types, and outcome metrics
  • Hole-card class (e.g. AKs, QQ)—not tied to your account in the aggregate store

We exclude from the statistics envelope (unless you separately share a hand):

  • Your account email and name
  • Free-text notes
  • Custom session names and precise venue text you type
  • Tags and other free-form labels

Aggregated statistics are stored so that other users cannot retrieve your individual hand from the stats row alone. Stats are keyed by a spot hash (a fingerprint of filter dimensions), not by your name.

You can turn off Contribute to community statistics in Settings → Privacy. When off, we do not add new contributions from your synced hands.

5.2 Sharing individual hands (separate, opt-in)

Sharing a hand is your explicit choice (per hand). If you share:

  • Other users may see a limited preview (format, position, result, etc.) according to product rules.
  • Hole cards and full action history may remain locked until another user purchases access (if that feature is enabled).
  • Your display name (not your email) may appear as the sharer.

You can unshare or delete a shared hand subject to technical caching and legal retention limits.
